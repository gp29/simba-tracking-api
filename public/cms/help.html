Account And Payment Option.

Que 1: I can’t sign in or request a ride

Sub Que 1: I can’t request a ride

Ans : If you can't request a ride, it could be for a few reasons:

  • You have an outstanding payment.
  • Your payment method was declined on a previous trip.
  • BKTrans is currently unavailable in your location.
  • There aren't any drivers in your area at this time. Please wait and try requesting again.
  • You haven't confirmed your email or phone number. Check for verification messages from BKTrans. If you don't have any, you may need to update your account settings.

Sub Que 2: I forgot my pasword

Ans : If you forget your password, visit the link below to reset. You'll need to enter your email address or mobile number associated with your BKTrans account.

You'll receive an email in a few minutes. This email includes a link to reset your password. The link can only be used once. If you do not open the link within 10 minutes, start over to receive a new link.

It's good practice to create a unique password for BKTrans. Be careful not to share your password in emails or other communication. BKTrans support will never ask you for your password.

If you are unable to reset your password or access your account, please get in touch with us at Account and Payment > Unable to Sign In > "My password reset link isn't working".

Sub Que 3: My password reset link isn’t working.

Ans : When you contact us to reset your BKTrans account password, you should receive an email within a few minutes. This email contains a link to help you reset your password.

For security, this link expires in 10 minutes. Open it immediately or wait until you're ready to complete the password reset process before requesting a password reset.

In the event that your password reset link doesn't work, please start over at BKTrans.com/forgot-password. Check that your email address is correct, then open the link in the next password reset email you receive.

If you're unable to reset your password or access your account, please get in touch with us below.

Que 2 : I can’t sign in to my account.

Ans : If you can't sign in to your account because you forgot your password, click the link below to reset your password.

Que 3: Trip Issues and Adjustments.

General Issue

Sub Que : 1 I was in accident.

Ans : If you are involved in an accident, please first make sure that all parties involved re safe. Contact the police and paramedics if necessary.

Next, please let us know what happened. Check that you have selected the correct trip and share all available info here. A member of our team will reach out to confirm everyone's safety and gather any other required information.

Issue with my fare.

Sub Que 1 : My fare was adjusted

Ans : Fare adjustments are sometimes made when riders reach out to us with concerns about their trips.

If your fare was adjusted after a recent trip, it may mean your rider reached out to us to request a fare review.

Common reasons for rider fare review requests are:

  • Trip didn't start or end on time
  • Wrong rider was picked up
  • Wrong rider was picked up
  • Route was not ideal
  • Technical issues impacted the trip
  • Driver refused the rider's destination

When an adjustment is made, we'll send you an email with the details of the updated fare.
